Corrections

Last updated: September 2026

We want our stories to be accurate. If you believe a STARTS JOURNEY article contains an error, please tell us.

How to report an error

Email editorial@startsjourney.example.com with the article title or link, the specific claim you believe is incorrect, and — where possible — a source that supports the correction.

What happens next

  • Our editorial team reviews the claim against our sourcing
  • If a correction is warranted, the article is updated and, for significant factual errors, a note is added to the story
  • We aim to review correction requests within a few business days

For claims that are disputed but not independently verifiable (such as a creator's stated follower count or a supplied biographical detail), we will review the language used to ensure it is clearly attributed rather than presented as independently confirmed.
