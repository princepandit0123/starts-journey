Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information STARTS JOURNEY collects from visitors to this site and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Email addresses submitted through our newsletter subscription form
  • Basic, non-identifying usage data (such as pages visited) that may be collected by standard web server logging
  • Content submitted through our story-submission or contact forms

How we use it

Newsletter email addresses are used only to send STARTS JOURNEY updates and are not sold to third parties. Submitted story content is reviewed by our editorial team before any publication decision is made.

Advertising

Advertisement placements on this site are clearly labelled. If a third-party advertising network is connected in the future, that network's own privacy practices would apply to any data it collects, and this policy would be updated accordingly.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, and you can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you.

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.
