About STARTS JOURNEY

Last updated: September 2026

STARTS JOURNEY is a digital-first entertainment and news publication covering Bollywood, Hollywood, OTT and web series, celebrity profiles, digital creators, reviews, box office tracking and Indian cricket.

Our goal is straightforward: report entertainment and creator-economy stories with context, accuracy and readable long-form writing, rather than reducing every story to a headline and a single image.

What we cover

  • Bollywood and Hollywood film news, first looks and trailers
  • OTT and web series releases and reviews
  • Profiles of digital creators, influencers and public figures
  • Indian cricket news and player profiles
  • Box office tracking and industry context

How we work

Some of our content is aggregated from public news feeds and linked back to the original publisher rather than republished in full. Editorial profiles are written by our team using publicly available information and, where noted, material supplied directly by the subject.

Questions about a story, a correction, or a partnership? Visit our Contact page.
